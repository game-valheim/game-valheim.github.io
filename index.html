Realtime Map from seed

Your running world on our map.

Realtime Map from seed connects your running Valheim game to the browser map at thevalheim.wiki. Follow your position and heading, see nearby creatures with their stars, and find players who share their position.

Version 1.5.1Requires BepInEx 5 and a desktop browser

Pick it here. Find it in Valheim.

Track a location, resource, portal, or pin. A personal in-game ping keeps the target and its distance in sight.

Keep the map beside your game

Use a desktop browser on the computer running Valheim. Nearby creatures appear within the area your game has loaded; the map does not reveal every creature or hidden player on a server.

From game to map

  1. Install BepInEx 5 and the Realtime Map from seed mod.
  2. Start Valheim and enter your world.
  3. Open the map, choose Connect game, and allow local-network access if your browser asks.
